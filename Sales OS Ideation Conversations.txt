One strategic suggestion
I would narrow the first version even further.
Don't try to support every kind of business immediately.
Start with digital products (ebooks, courses, templates, PDFs) because the workflow is relatively simple:
1. Customer arrives from an ad.
2. AI greets them naturally (including Nigerian English or Pidgin where appropriate).
3. AI identifies what they want or recommends a product from the catalog.
4. AI handles common objections and answers questions.
5. AI shares the payment link.
6. AI verifies payment (through your chosen payment integration or confirmation flow).
7. AI delivers the digital product instantly.
8. AI updates the CRM automatically.
9. AI schedules personalized follow-ups for abandoned conversations.
10. AI escalates to a human only when necessary.
If you can reliably automate that end-to-end flow, you'll have solved a real, high-value problem. From there, expanding to physical products, bookings, services, or other industries becomes much easier because the core "AI sales rep" engine already exists.
This also aligns well with your broader thinking around Antflow. Rather than another generic chatbot, you're building an AI employee that owns a business outcome—closing sales—using conversation, tools, workflow automation, and memory. That's a more defensible product than competing on chat alone.
